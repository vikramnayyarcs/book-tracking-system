This video shows how to add a book to your shelf.
All data in this test run is seeded test data.
The test reader opens the Add a book page.
The page has three ways to find a book.
The reader can push Scan barcode and point the camera at the barcode on the book.
The browser asks for permission to use the camera.
The reader can also type the ISBN.
In this video, the reader types the ISBN and pushes Look up.
The page shows the title, the author and the number of pages.
The reader can change these details.
The reader pushes Add to shelf.
The page shows the new book.
If the ISBN is not valid, the page tells the reader.
If the camera does not work, the page tells the reader to type the ISBN.
The reader can also search by title or author, or enter the details by hand.
If the device is offline, the buttons that need a connection are off and the page tells the reader why.

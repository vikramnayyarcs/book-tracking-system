This video shows how to save a quote from a photo of a book page.
All data in this test run is seeded test data.
The page in the photo is an invented page. It is not from a real book.
First, the reader opens a book.
The Quotes section has a button, Add a quote from a photo.
On a phone, the button opens the camera.
The reader takes a photo of the page.
The app reads the text on the photo.
The app deletes the photo after it reads the text.
The page shows the lines of text, one line at a time.
The reader selects the lines of the passage.
The reader types the page number.
The reader pushes Save quote.
The page shows the new quote with its page number.
If the photo has no text, the page tells the reader to take a new photo.
If the reading fails, the page shows a message. The reader takes a new photo.
If the device is offline, the button is off and the page tells the reader why.
The app never logs the photo or the text.

This video shows how to add a book that you finished before you used the app.
All data in this test run is seeded test data.
The test reader opens the Add a book page.
The reader enters the details of the book by hand.
The reader pushes Finished before.
The page asks for the finish date.
The reader does not need the exact day.
The reader can give the month and the year, or only the year.
In this video, the reader gives March 2019.
The reader gives the book a rating of 4.
The reader can also add a photo.
The reader writes a note about the book.
The reader can record a voice note instead.
The reader pushes Add past book.
The page shows that the book is added.
The app turns the note into insights.
The page shows the insights.
The reader pushes Give me 3 recall prompts.
The page shows 3 questions that help the reader to remember the book.
The reader can write an answer to each question.
If the device is offline, the button is off and the page tells the reader why.

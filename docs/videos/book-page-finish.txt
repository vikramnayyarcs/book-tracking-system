This video shows the book page and how to finish a book.
All data in this test run is seeded test data.
The test reader opens the shelf and taps the title of a book.
The book page opens.
The page shows the sessions for this book.
Each session shows the date and the pages that the reader read.
The page also shows the insights and the quotes.
The page shows three reflection questions, but only after the reader asks for them.
The reader taps Ask me 3 questions.
The app writes the questions from the notes of the reader.
The reader types an answer and saves it.
Now the reader taps Finish book.
The finish page opens.
The reader chooses a rating from 1 to 5.
The reader can add a photo and write a review.
The reader can also answer the 3 questions for the end of the book.
The reader taps Finish book again.
The book page shows the status Finished.
The page also shows the rating and the review.
Finishing a book needs an internet connection.
